Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap a mirror to turn it. The sunlight bounces off mirrors; every lantern it touches starts to glow. Light every lantern to finish the level.

I'm stuck. Is this level possible?

Yes. Every level is checked by a solver before release and has exactly one solution. Tap Hint: a glowing ring shows the next mirror on the light's true path. Undo is always free.

How do stars work?

Solve in "par" taps (the fewest possible) for 3 stars. Every turn counts, including undo, so stars stay honest. There's no penalty for taking your time.

I bought the Supporter Pack on another device.

Open Settings → Support Lanternwise → Restore purchases. Family Sharing is supported.

Are there ads?

No. Lanternwise has no ads and no subscriptions. The optional Supporter Pack adds three extra light colors; every level plays the same with or without it.

Does it work offline?

Yes, all levels and the daily puzzle work without internet.

Accessibility

Lanternwise supports VoiceOver, Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion, and has an optional "Tap twice to turn" mode in Settings to prevent accidental taps.
