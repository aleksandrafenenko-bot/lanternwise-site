Privacy Policy

Lanternwise · Last updated: November 13, 2026

Lanternwise is made by Oleksandra Fenenko ("we"). We built Lanternwise to respect your privacy: there are no accounts, and we don't collect your personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress, stars, hints, daily-puzzle streak and settings are saved only on your iPhone (and shared with the Lanternwise widget on the same device). We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We receive no payment details; the app only checks with Apple whether you own the Supporter Pack.

No ads, no tracking

Lanternwise shows no ads, contains no advertising or analytics code, and doesn't track you.

Children

Lanternwise is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We'll update this page if anything changes. Questions: oleksandra.fenenko@gmail.com.
